`*-cut.png` files were made with `scripts/treat.py cutout`. Most originals are small (512–1600 px; the eagle is 1826 × 2019): treat them (halftone, photocopy, posterize) or keep them small in the layout.

Real people: the set used to include a NASA portrait of the astronaut Eileen Collins. It was removed because using a well-known person's likeness to promote an event implies endorsement. The cameraman is an unnamed subject whose photo was released CC0; for anything published, prefer the user's own photos of people, with their consent.
